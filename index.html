About the Hillsborough Arts Council

The Hillsborough Arts Council (HAC) has long been a gathering place for artists, makers, and art lovers in Orange County, North Carolina. Nestled in the historic town of Hillsborough, the HAC Gallery & Gift Shop provides a vibrant venue for regional artists to share their handcrafted work with the community and visitors alike.

The Gallery & Gift Shop

The HAC Gallery & Gift Shop showcases original works by consigning artists who live and work primarily in the greater Hillsborough and Orange County area. From wheel-thrown pottery and forged metalwork to hand-painted silk and botanical jewelry, the gallery reflects the rich diversity of craft traditions and contemporary art practices found in this part of North Carolina.

A Living Tradition

The artists you see here carry on a long legacy of making — drawing on traditions from blacksmithing and woodturning to stained glass and textile art — while bringing their own voices, stories, and innovations to each piece. Many are rooted in Hillsborough itself; others travel from across the region to be part of this community. All share a commitment to handcrafted work made with care and intention.
